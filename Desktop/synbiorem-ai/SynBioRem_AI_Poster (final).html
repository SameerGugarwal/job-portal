Problem Definition

Environmental pollutants — heavy metals, hydrocarbons, dyes, pesticides — resist conventional remediation. Biological approaches depend on pollutant type, organism suitability, pH, temperature, cost, and safety — variables too complex for manual trial-and-error. No unified intelligent tool exists to recommend, design, and simulate engineered microbial solutions at scale.

Proposed Solution

SynBioRem AI is an intelligent recommendation platform that ingests a user-defined pollution scenario and outputs ranked synthetic microbe designs with mechanism blueprints, safety evaluations, and deployment strategies. Users interactively tune pH, temperature, and concentration to observe real-time changes in remediation efficiency through a dynamic simulation layer.

Scoring Formula
Final Score =
  0.35 × Remediation Match
+ 0.20 × Environment Compatibility
+ 0.20 × Safety Score
+ 0.10 × Cost Efficiency
+ 0.15 × Scalability Index
How It Works
  • User defines a pollution scenario via interactive sliders and dropdowns
  • Pollutant profiler classifies the input and determines the required remediation mechanism
  • Knowledge base supplies organism–pollutant mappings, environmental ranges, and safety data
  • AI engine applies rule-based filtering + weighted multi-criteria scoring to rank candidates
  • Blueprint generator outputs a synthetic design card: chassis, pathway, kill switch, deployment
  • Simulation layer models concentration reduction over time as users adjust parameters
