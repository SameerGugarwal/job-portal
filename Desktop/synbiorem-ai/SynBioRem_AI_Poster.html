Proposed Solution

SynBioRem AI is an intelligent decision-support platform that recommends, blueprints, and scores synthetic microbe designs for site-specific pollutant remediation.

  • Input: Pollutant type, concentration, site conditions
  • AI Engine: Matches pollutant profile → optimal organism + metabolic pathway
  • Blueprint: Genetic circuit design with promoter & gene recommendations
  • Scoring: Safety index, ecological risk, scalability & containment rating
  • Output: Deployment strategy + regulatory checklist

💡 Innovation: First platform to integrate synthetic biology design + AI scoring + real-world deployment logistics in a unified dashboard.

How It Works
1
Pollutant Intake: User submits pollutant type, concentration & site metadata via Streamlit UI or REST API
2
Knowledge Retrieval: Structured KB queries return candidate organisms, gene circuits & known metabolic pathways
3
AI Matching: Weighted scoring model ranks organism–pollutant pairs on efficacy, safety & ecological compatibility
4
Blueprint Generation: Auto-generates synthetic gene circuit design with promoters, reporters & kill-switches
5
Deployment Plan: Recommends containment strategy, field inoculation method & regulatory biosafety checklist
